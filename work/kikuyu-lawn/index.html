Seasons

A Highveld lawn, season by season

Winter · June to August

Frost, and a lawn that's gone to sleep

Kikuyu goes brown and dormant after the first frost. We don't scalp it. We clean up, cut back what's frost-bitten and sort out the edges, so spring starts from a tidy yard.

Spring · September to November

When the jacarandas flower, the lawn wakes up

This is the month that decides your summer. We scarify the kikuyu, top-dress the thin patches and give it the first feed of the year.

Summer · December to February

Afternoon storms, and growth every single day

Highveld rain means the grass never stops. The Standard plan's weekly cut earns its keep here, and we pull the weeds before they seed.

Autumn · March to May

Leaves down, lawn slowing, beds put to bed

The last feed before winter, leaves cleared every visit, and beds mulched so the roots are ready for the frost.
