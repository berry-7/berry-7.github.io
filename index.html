The things people ask before they book.

01How long does it actually take?

Most sites go live within 7 days of me having your content. Bigger builds take a little longer, and you get a firm date up front either way.

02What do you need from me?

Your logo, a few photos, and a rough idea of your services. If none of that is ready, the audit call sorts it out. I will tell you exactly what to send.

03What if I don’t like it?

Two rounds of changes are included. If you still don’t love it, you get the full refund. That is the whole point of paying upfront.

04Can I update it myself afterwards?

Yes. It is built in Framer, so text, prices and photos are yours to change without a developer.

05What does the monthly fee cover?

Hosting, security, backups, small updates and support after launch. Skip it if you would rather handle hosting yourself.

06What if I don’t have any photos?

Common, and not a problem. I source what is needed for the build and tell you exactly which few shots to take on your phone.
